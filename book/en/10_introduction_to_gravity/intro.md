# Introduction to Gravity

This chapter is based on the content from "ApuntesGrvTema1.pdf" and provides an introduction to Newtonian gravitation, covering:

- Newton's Universal Law of Gravitation
- Historical context and development
- Successes and limitations of Newtonian theory
- Incompatibility with special relativity
- Tidal forces

The content describes the fundamental principles of gravitational physics, from Newton's groundbreaking work in the 17th century through the observations and astronomical predictions that validated the theory, and finally the limitations that led Einstein to develop the theory of general relativity in 1915.

Key topics include:
- The law of universal gravitation and the gravitational constant
- The discovery of Neptune and tests of Newtonian predictions
- The anomalous precession of Mercury's perihelion
- The transition from Newtonian gravity to Einstein's general relativity
- Introduction to gravitational fields and the concept of tidal forces
