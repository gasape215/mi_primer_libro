# Summary of the paper “Transverse expansion of the metric at null infinity"

This chapter summarizes the article available in `resources/Paper_scri.pdf`, written by Marc Mars and Gabriel Sánchez-Pérez.

## Objective

The paper analyzes the conformal Einstein equations at null infinity (_I_) to all orders without imposing restrictions on spacetime dimension, the topology of _I_, or the fall-off conditions for the Weyl tensor.

## Main results

- It identifies the free data at null infinity that determine the asymptotic geometry.
- It proves that two asymptotically flat spacetimes with the same free data at _I_ are isometric to infinite order.
- It establishes an existence theorem for asymptotically flat spacetimes realizing prescribed initial data at _I_.
- It introduces a detached definition of null infinity that does not rely on a preexisting spacetime.

## Method

- A coordinate-free approach is used.
- The conformal factor Ω is treated as a dynamical variable.
- The paper uses null hypersurface data and the transverse expansion of the metric.

## Obstacles and contributions

- Two geometric obstructions appear: the Coulombian obstruction tensor and the radiative obstruction tensor.
- The free data structure depends on spacetime dimension and parity.
- This work extends classical Bondi-style analyses and Fefferman–Graham expansions to non-spherical topologies and general dimensions.

## Relevance

The chapter highlights that the geometry of null infinity can be described in a much more general conformal framework, which is useful for studying gravitational radiation and asymptotically flat fields in diverse settings.
